Project Overview

This project analyzes customer spending and sales performance for Goodness Fashion House using Microsoft Excel. The goal was to understand revenue trends, customer purchasing behavior, and the performance of different customer acquisition channels and locations to support data-driven business decisions.

Tools Used: Microsoft Excel, PivotTables, Charts, KPI Analysis, Interactive Dashboard

Dataset: 100 customers and 478 orders.

Key Findings
- Total Revenue: ₦18,515,857 across 478 orders.
- Customer Acquisition: WhatsApp generated the highest revenue among the analyzed acquisition channels, contributing approximately ₦4.18 million.
- Gender Analysis: Female customers accounted for approximately ₦15.41 million in spending.
- Location Analysis: Benin City recorded the highest revenue among the analyzed locations, at approximately ₦3.87 million.
- Sales Trends: Monthly revenue patterns were examined to identify changes in performance and opportunities for improvement.
Business Recommendations

The analysis highlighted opportunities to strengthen WhatsApp marketing, develop targeted campaigns for high-value customer segments, improve location-specific promotions, and monitor monthly revenue trends to support sales planning.

Outcome

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to present key performance indicators, customer spending patterns, acquisition channel performance, geographic revenue distribution, and monthly sales trends in one view, making it easier to monitor performance and identify opportunities for growth.
