# Gs_Couture – Fashion Retail Sales & Customer Analysis

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Power BI, DAX, Power Query

**Project Type:** Data Analysis | Retail Analytics | Customer Behaviour Analysis

## Project Overview

Gs_Couture is a fashion retail business offering dresses, two-piece sets, skirts, jumpsuits, tops, and trousers.

This project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing behaviour, sales performance, revenue trends, and product preferences to uncover actionable business insights. Using Microsoft Excel and Power BI, I transformed a retail dataset into an interactive dashboard that provides a clear view of business performance and supports data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis were to:

- Evaluate overall sales and revenue performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour and preferences.
- Identify the best-performing product categories.
- Analyze revenue trends across different periods.
- Examine customer segments and purchasing patterns.
- Identify opportunities to improve customer retention and sales performance.

## Data Preparation & Analysis

I used Microsoft Excel to organize and prepare the dataset for analysis. The preparation process included reviewing data quality, checking inconsistencies, standardizing fields, and ensuring the dataset was suitable for visualization.

I then used Power BI and DAX to develop interactive visualizations and performance metrics, allowing users to explore sales performance and customer trends from different perspectives.

## Key Areas of Analysis

1. **Sales Performance:** Evaluated revenue and purchasing activity across the business.
2. **Product Performance:** Examined customer preferences and the performance of different fashion categories.
3. **Customer Analysis:** Explored customer segments, demographics, and purchasing behaviour.
4. **Revenue Trends:** Analyzed sales patterns over time to identify periods of increased or decreased purchasing activity.
5. **Customer Retention:** Examined purchasing recency to identify customers who may require targeted engagement.

## Business Value & Recommendations

The dashboard provides management with insights that can support:

- Better inventory planning based on product demand.
- Targeted marketing campaigns for different customer segments.
- Customer retention strategies to encourage repeat purchases.
- Improved sales monitoring and performance tracking.
- More informed decisions about product promotions and business growth.

## Project Deliverables

- Excel dataset preparation and analysis.
- Interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Business insights and recommendations.

**Conclusion**

This project demonstrates my ability to transform retail data into meaningful business insights using Excel and Power BI. It highlights how data analytics can help fashion retailers understand their customers, monitor performance, and make informed business decisions.
